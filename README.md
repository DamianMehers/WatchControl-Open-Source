This is an open source copy of my WatchControl app that I released and then eventually pulled from the store.

**Search for "TBS" in the project files for places where you'll need to use your own code**

Use WatchControl to control your Mac's audio playback directly from your Apple Watch (Play/Pause, Mute/Unmute etc.).

It uses bluetooth to talk directly to to the Mac. No network. No accounts. No logging in.
 

Step 1 Install the Apple Watch app via your iPhone .

Watch App Searching Watch App Connecting Watch App Connected

Step 2 Install a tiny Mac menu-bar companion app on your Mac

There is no step 3.

Both devices must be signed into the same iCloud account with KeyChain sharing enabled (used to initially securely share bluetooth connection info from the Mac app to the Watch app).
